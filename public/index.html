Safety & Verification

The Intermediary Agency Advantage

Traditional car rentals are bloated with overhead and excessive counter fees. Pure peer-to-peer sharing often lacks reliability. RentPeer strikes the perfect balance by employing an active Intermediary Agency to oversee every interaction:

  • ✓ Driver Verification: Manual and automated KYC checks for clean driving records.
  • ✓ Vehicle Documentation: Host registration, insurance policy, and roadworthiness approvals.
  • ✓ Escrow Protection: Renter payments and security deposits are held in trust until return.
  • ✓ Dispute Mediation: Independent arbitration for any damage or cancellation claims.
